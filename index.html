About Me
Toki!

I'm Penny, a full-time software developer with a background in astrophysics. I made this website so I can show off some of the cool stuff I've done.

I'm currently working in the UK as a software engineer. I do most of my professional work in the field of metrology, being involved with various aerospace, automotive, and pretty much whatever you can think of that needs precision manufacturing.

I also work on my own projects in my free time. I'm still carrying on from my computational physics background making sims of various sorts. Because I'm full of myself, I like to think that I'm a bit of a game designer and have worked on a few different game concepts since I learned how to write code. I hope one day to be able to unite my two loves and make a fully physics simulated game which is actually fun to play. As hard as that may be (and Outer Wilds beat me to it anyway).

I previously graduated with a First Class Master's Degree in physics with astrophysics from Northumbria University. This is where I found my love of programming (which I still dont know why everyone else in the class, bar one, hated). My masters and bachelors disserations can be viewed at the right hand side.
